              Tips for play time and badges
              • Play time only counts for games Discord detects by exe. Rich Presence entries (apps, mobile games) don’t count.
              • Discord records play time when it detects the game, every 30 minutes after that, and when the game closes, so keep Discord open.
              • In Discord, turn on Settings → Activity Privacy → “Share your detected activities with others”.
              • The game time, game variety and streaming badges also need the personalization setting on, under Settings → Data & Privacy.
              • Discord keeps badges for up to an hour before checking again. Ctrl+R in Discord reloads it.
